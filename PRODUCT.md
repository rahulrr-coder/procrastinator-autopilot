# Product

register: product

## Users
People who keep avoiding a task and feel guilty about it. Used on a phone or laptop, often in the evening, tired, low on willpower. They want to be unblocked, not coached.

## Product purpose
The user admits what they are avoiding. Autopilot classifies it (habit, petty task, important deadline), diagnoses why they are slacking, and gives a 5-second first move plus small steps grounded in named behavioural principles. It remembers open tasks so nothing is forgotten, and gets more careful when the user is overloaded.

## Tone
Warm, dry, non-judgmental. Never hustle culture. Short sentences. No guilt, no exclamation marks, no em dashes.

## Anti-references
Neon-on-black productivity dashboards, streak-shaming gamification, wellness sage-and-cream, multicolor gradient blobs, stat-card grids.

## Strategic principles
- One thing at a time. Focus screens hide everything else.
- Starting must feel cheaper than not starting.
- Honest about evidence: show how strong each principle's research is.
